[h1]BETTER NATIONALIZATION[/h1]

[i]This mod has 2 functions:
[list]
[*]Remove infamy for the war goal of forcing nationalization.
[*]Enable nationalization even if you've enacted [b]Laissez Faire[/b].
[/list][/i]
